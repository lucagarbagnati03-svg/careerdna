# CareerDNA UI/UX QA Analysis

## 🔴 Critical Issues

### 1. Content Cutoff / Text Truncation
**Location:** Bottom of the scrollable policy card (Data Sensitivity section)
**Issue:** Item #2 "Confidentiality & NDA" is visibly cut mid-sentence at the bottom of the modal. Users cannot tell there is more content without scrolling, and there's **no scroll indicator** (fade gradient, scrollbar, or "scroll to read more" hint).
**Impact:** Users may accept terms without reading them fully — a **legal/compliance risk**.

---

## 🟠 High Issues

### 2. No Scroll Affordance Indicator
**Location:** Both policy text boxes
**Issue:** Neither scrollable container has a visible scrollbar, scroll shadow/fade, or any affordance to indicate scrollability. The cutoff text is the only hint.
**Recommendation:** Add a bottom fade gradient overlay + visible scrollbar.

### 3. "I Accept & Continue" Button Accessibility
**Location:** Bottom CTA button
**Issue:** No **checkbox or explicit acknowledgment mechanism** before acceptance. Best practice (and often legally required) is a checkbox: *"I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service"* before enabling the button.

### 4. Missing Disabled State Logic
**Location:** CTA Button
**Issue:** The button appears **immediately active** (full purple) even though content is cut off and hasn't been fully read. Consider disabling until user scrolls to bottom.

---

## 🟡 Medium Issues

### 5. Inconsistent Card Styling
**Location:** Two content boxes
**Issue:**
- First box: subtle border, lighter background
- Second box (warning): amber left border + darker background
- The **border-radius and padding appear inconsistent** between the two cards

### 6. Warning Icon Rendering
**Location:** "⚠ Data Sensitivity" header
**Issue:** The warning emoji (⚠️) may render inconsistently across platforms/OS. Should be replaced with an **SVG icon** for consistency.

### 7. Link Styling Contrast
**Location:** "Privacy Policy · Terms of Service" footer links
**Issue:** The links appear in a **muted purple/gray** against the dark background. Contrast ratio likely **fails WCAG AA** (requires 4.5:1 for normal text).

### 8. Bullet Point Alignment
**Location:** First policy card, bullet list
**Issue:** Bullet points (•) appear to have **inconsistent left padding** compared to the numbered list in the second card. Mixed list styles (bullets vs numbers) without clear visual hierarchy.

---

## 🔵 Low Issues

### 9. Logo/Icon Sizing
**Location:** Header — CareerDNA logo
**Issue:** The rocket/DNA emoji icon appears **slightly small** relative to the bold title text. Minor visual imbalance.

### 10. Separator Between Footer Links
**Location:** "Privacy Policy · Terms of Service"
**Issue:** The **dot separator (·)** has inconsistent spacing — appears tighter on one side. Should use equal margins.

### 11. Modal Container Top Clipping
**Location:** Top edge of the modal card
**Issue:** The modal appears to start at the very **top edge** of the visible screen with minimal top margin, suggesting potential clipping on smaller devices.

### 12. No Close/Cancel Option
**Location:** Modal — globally
**Issue:** There is **no X button or Cancel option**. Users are forced to accept with no clear exit path, which may violate UX best practices and app store guidelines.

### 13. Font Weight Inconsistency
**Location:** Body text throughout
**Issue:** Mixed use of bold labels inline with regular text. The **bold terms** (Account email address, CV/Resume text, Journal entries) are consistent, but the description text weight feels lighter than optimal for dark-mode readability.
